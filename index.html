About

Meet Shirley

Shirley is a makeup artist based in Eight Mile Plains, Brisbane, with a love for soft, elegant looks that photograph beautifully and feel comfortable to wear. What started as doing makeup for friends and family grew into a workshop studio where every client gets unhurried, personal attention.

She believes the best makeup never masks you — it highlights you. Whether it's your wedding day or a special night out, you'll walk out of her studio feeling calm, confident and radiant.

"My goal is simple — you should still look like you, just at your very best."

— Shirley
